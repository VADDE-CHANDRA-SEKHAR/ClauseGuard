MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of March 1, 2025 ("Effective Date") between
Northwind Analytics Inc. ("Vendor") and Customer.

Term: This Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial term of twelve (12) months
from the Effective Date ("Initial Term"), expiring February 28, 2026.

Auto-Renewal: This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month
terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least twenty (20)
days prior to the end of the then-current term. Failure to provide timely notice will
result in automatic renewal at a 12% price increase over the prior term's fees.

Termination: Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause upon thirty (30) days
written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision and fails to cure
such breach within the notice period.
